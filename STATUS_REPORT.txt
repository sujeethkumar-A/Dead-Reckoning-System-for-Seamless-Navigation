📱 HOW TO USE ON ANDROID PHONE:

1. Find your laptop's LAN IP:
   Get-NetIPAddress -AddressFamily IPv4 | ?{$_.PrefixOrigin -eq "Dhcp"}
   (Example: 10.199.80.175)

2. Open Chrome on phone:
   http://10.199.80.175:5500/sensor.html

3. Set backend URL:
   http://10.199.80.175:8000

4. Tap "CONNECT SENSORS"

5. Grant permissions:
   - Location (GPS)
   - Motion sensors

6. Watch real sensor data stream!
